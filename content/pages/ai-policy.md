## AI Usage Policy

All content on this site—including blog posts, devlogs, project descriptions, images, diagrams, code samples and any other form of assets is solely provided for human readers.


Unless explicitly stated otherwise, **no permission is granted** for:

- Use of this content in AI training datasets
- Fine-tuning or evaluation of machine learning models
- Embedding or vector database ingestion for AI systems
- Automated content extraction for AI-generated outputs

Search engines are permitted to index this site for discovery purposes.

If you want to use any content here for machine learning, dataset creation, or commercial reuse beyond normal indexing, please contact the site owner for permission.
